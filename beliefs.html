What We Believe

The essential truths that guide our faith and ministry.

1. The Holy Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible word of God.

Read full statement & Scripture
We believe that the Holy Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments are the complete, written Word of God, fully inspired by the Holy Spirit, without error in the original manuscripts, and the supreme authority in all matters of faith and life. Key Passages: 2 Timothy 3:16-17, 2 Peter 1:20-21

2. God & The Trinity

We believe in one eternal God existing in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Read full statement & Scripture
We believe in one God, creator of all things, infinitely perfect, and eternally existing in three distinct Persons: God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit, each equal in power and glory. Key Passages: Deuteronomy 6:4, Matthew 28:19, 2 Corinthians 13:14

3. Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, who died for our sins and rose again.

Read full statement & Scripture
We believe Jesus Christ was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, lived a sinless life, died on the cross as a sacrifice for our sins, arose bodily from the dead, and ascended into heaven where He reigns as Lord. Key Passages: John 1:1-14, 1 Corinthians 15:3-4, Philippians 2:5-11

4. Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is a free gift from God received through faith in Jesus Christ.

Read full statement & Scripture
We believe all people have sinned and fallen short of God’s glory. Salvation cannot be earned by good works; it is given solely by God's grace through repentance and faith in the finished work of Jesus Christ on the cross. Key Passages: Ephesians 2:8-9, Romans 3:23, John 14:6

5. The Church & Community

We believe the Church is called to gather for worship, fellowship, and serving others.

Read full statement & Scripture
We believe the Church consists of all true believers in Jesus Christ. The local church is called to assemble for prayer, teaching, the ordinances of baptism and communion, and sharing the Gospel with the world. Key Passages: Acts 2:42-47, Hebrews 10:24-25, Matthew 28:18-20
