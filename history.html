Our History & Heritage

Looking back at God's faithfulness throughout our church journey.

A Legacy of Faith

For decades, our church family has been dedicated to proclaiming the gospel, growing together in God's Word, and serving our local community. We praise God for all He has done in our past and look forward with anticipation to where He is leading us next.

Key Milestones

1975

Church Founded

A small group of believers began gathering in a local home for weekly prayer and Bible study, laying the spiritual foundation for our congregation.

1982

First Building Dedicated

As the congregation grew, the church acquired property and built its first sanctuary to accommodate Sunday services and Sunday school classes.

2005

Ministry & Campus Expansion

Added new classroom spaces and a fellowship hall to better serve families, expand youth programs, and host community outreach events.

Present Day

Continuing the Mission

Today, our desire remains the same: to glorify God through sound teaching, authentic fellowship, and sharing the love of Christ in our town and beyond.
